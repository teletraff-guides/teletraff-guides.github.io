Automation saves a launch scenario and repeats it on a schedule or on a condition, so you watch
a log instead of starting the job by hand every time.

The section overview lives on the [automation page](https://teletraff.com/en/modules/automation).

## What you need beforehand

- **A task you have already run manually** and whose result you understand.
- **Free accounts at the moment it fires.** A schedule does not create accounts — if the fleet
  is busy at that hour, the run will not happen.

<Aside type="caution" title="By hand first">
Automation repeats a scenario, it does not check whether the scenario makes sense. A run that did
not work manually will keep not working on a schedule — only now regularly and without your
attention.
</Aside>

## How to set it up

<Steps>

1. **Choose a supported task and its parameters.**

   <figure>
     <img src="/screenshots/automation-wizard.webp" alt="TeleTraff automation wizard with task selection, trigger conditions and schedule" width="1264" height="1080" loading="lazy" />
     <figcaption>Automation wizard: choosing the task, trigger conditions and schedule.</figcaption>

2. **Set the conditions and the schedule.**

3. **Check the configuration** before enabling the scenario.

4. **Enable it and watch the run log.**

   Every firing appears as a separate entry — with its result, not just the fact that it started.

</Steps>

## What is worth automating

| Scenario | Why it fits |
| --- | --- |
| Regular [warmup](/en/modules/account-warmup/) of the reserve | preparation needs no involvement from you |
| Periodic [dataset collection](/en/modules/parsers/) | the audience refreshes without a manual start |
| Watching for new posts | this needs a continuous rather than one-off mode |

One-off campaigns where you check the result by hand are usually not worth automating: the saving
does not offset the risk of repeating a mistake many times over.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Automating an unverified scenario | the mistake repeats regularly and unobserved |
| A schedule that ignores fleet occupation | the run does not happen, the accounts are busy |
| Firing too often | the load on accounts grows faster than the result |
| Enable and forget | the log exists precisely so that someone looks at it |

## Limits and platform rules

Regularity makes automation more visible to the platform than a one-off run: identical actions at
the same time form a recognisable pattern. Telegram's rules apply exactly as they do to a manual
launch. See [Security](/en/security/).

## FAQ

**Which tasks can be scheduled?**
The panel shows the supported tasks during selection. The list depends on the module.

**What if the accounts are busy when it fires?**
The run does not happen — a schedule does not free accounts. This is visible in the log.

**Can a scenario be disabled without deleting it?**
Yes, a scenario is enabled and disabled separately from its configuration.

**Where can I see what happened?**
In the run log: every firing is recorded with its result.
