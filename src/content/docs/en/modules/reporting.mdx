The module prepares submissions to Telegram about a single target or a sequence of targets from
a dataset, with a category and a saved result.

The section overview lives on the [report log page](https://teletraff.com/en/modules/reporter).

<Aside type="caution" title="Read this before configuring anything">
The module is intended for reports about **genuine violations**: fraud, content theft, someone
impersonating your brand. Mass reports against legitimate resources are abuse that harms your own
accounts first and does not produce the result you might expect.

A report is a submission to the platform, not a tool for influencing competitors.
</Aside>

## What you need before launch

- **Accounts with proxies.**
- **A specific target** or a dataset of targets.
- **Grounds.** The report category must match what is actually happening.

## What the module does not do

- **It does not delete channels or accounts.** Telegram makes that decision, and the panel does not
  influence it.
- **It does not guarantee an outcome.** A submitted report is a submission, not a result.
- **It does not bypass platform limits.**

A result in the log means “the report was submitted”, not “the target was blocked”.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| A category that does not match the substance | the submission is not considered on its merits |
| Mass reports against legitimate resources | your accounts suffer, the target stays put |
| Expecting a block as the run's result | the log records the submission, not the platform's decision |

## Limits, user consent and platform rules

This is the one module in the panel where abuse causes direct harm to third parties.

- Submit reports only when there are genuine grounds.
- False reports violate Telegram's rules and can result in restrictions on your own accounts.
- In a number of jurisdictions a knowingly false report aimed at damaging someone's business
  carries legal consequences.
- Responsibility for the content of a report lies with the operator.

See [Responsible use](/en/responsible-use/).

## FAQ

**Will a report lead to a block?**
Telegram decides. The panel records the fact of the submission, not its outcome.

**Can I report several targets at once?**
Yes, working from a dataset of targets is supported.

**How many accounts should be involved?**
As many as correspond to the real number of people who have grounds to report. Volume on its own
adds nothing to the result.
