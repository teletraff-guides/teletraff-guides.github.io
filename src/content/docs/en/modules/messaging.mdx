Messaging sends a prepared message to a chosen source of recipients from your accounts. The text
can be written as several variants, personalised with recipient data and accompanied by media.

The module overview lives on the [messaging page](https://teletraff.com/en/modules/mailing).

## What you need before launch

- **Accounts with proxies**, preferably [warmed up](/en/modules/account-warmup/) — messaging loads
  an account harder than most modules.
- **A permitted source of recipients**: a collected dataset, contacts, open dialogues of the
  selected accounts, or available chats.
- **Prepared text** — ideally in several variants.
- **A clear reason why these people expect to hear from you.** This is not a formality, see
  [responsible use](/en/responsible-use/).

## How to launch

<Steps>

1. **Select accounts and the source of recipients.**

   The source can be a dataset from [parsers](/en/modules/parsers/), contacts, open dialogues
   of the selected accounts, or available chats. The exact recipient list is resolved right before
   sending, so the final count may differ from the dataset size.

   <figure>
     <img src="/screenshots/mailing-setup.webp" alt="TeleTraff messaging setup screen with recipients, message variants, media and limits" width="1264" height="1080" loading="lazy" />
     <figcaption>Messaging settings: recipient source, message variants, attachments and the per-account limit.</figcaption>
   </figure>

2. **Prepare the message variants.**

   There can be several. For each recipient **one** variant is chosen, then their data is inserted.

   <Aside type="tip" title="Why several variants">
   Identical text going out from every account is the clearest sign of a bulk send — both to the
   recipient and to anti-spam. Several variants solve that more cheaply than any technical trick.
   </Aside>

3. **Set up personalisation and the link.**

   Recipient data is inserted into the text. The link is a separate field and goes into the message
   by substitution.

   **AI text rewriting** is available: the model rewrites a variant while preserving names,
   personal data and the link.

4. **Attach media if needed.**

   Photos and video, audio files, documents and recorded voice messages are supported.

5. **Set limits and check the plan.**

   “Messages per account” sets the load on a single session. The panel builds a pre-launch plan
   showing how many recipients fall to each account.

6. **Decide whether you need auto-reply.**

   The “reply to an incoming message” toggle lets the account continue a dialogue it started, after
   a confirmed send. The reply text is set in advance.

7. **Start the job and review delivery statuses.**

</Steps>

## A safe test run

<Steps>

1. Take **one warmed-up account** and a dataset of **a few of your own contacts** who know
   about it in advance.
2. Prepare two text variants and enable personalisation.
3. Send, then check: data substitution worked, the link is in place, each person got a different variant.
4. Look at the delivery statuses — you will see the result format before a real campaign.

</Steps>

## Why a message may not arrive

- The recipient has closed direct messages from people they do not know.
- The account hit its session limit or is waiting out a pause between sends.
- Telegram asked to wait (FloodWait) — the thread pauses.
- The recipient appeared in the dataset twice — no repeat send is performed.
- The account received a restriction and stopped in this job.

Status details are covered in [troubleshooting](/en/troubleshooting/).

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| One text variant for the whole dataset | obvious to recipients and to anti-spam |
| Messaging from accounts that were not warmed up | restrictions arrive fastest |
| The whole volume on one account | the load is not spread across the fleet |
| A link in the very first message to a stranger | the most common trigger for complaints |

## Limits, user consent and platform rules

Messaging is the most sensitive module in the panel because it writes to people directly.

- Write to those who expect to hear from you: customers, subscribers, members of your community.
- **Presence in a collected dataset is not consent** to receive messages.
- Leave a clear way to opt out, and honour it.
- Data-protection and marketing law applies regardless of what the panel can technically do.
- Telegram's rules take precedence over job settings; bulk sending to strangers leads to complaints
  and account restrictions.

See [Responsible use](/en/responsible-use/).

## FAQ

**Can different people get different text?**
Yes. There can be several variants, and one is chosen per recipient.

**What does AI rewriting do?**
It rewrites your variant while preserving names, personal data and the link.

**Is the link preserved after rewriting?**
Yes, the link and personal data are preserved.

**Can I attach a voice message?**
Yes, it can be recorded in the panel, and ready audio files can be attached too.

**What is auto-reply?**
After a confirmed send the account can answer an incoming message with pre-set text, so the
dialogue does not break off.

**Why does the recipient count differ from the dataset size?**
The list is resolved before sending: duplicates and unreachable recipients are filtered out.
