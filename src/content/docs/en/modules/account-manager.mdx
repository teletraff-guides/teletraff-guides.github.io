The account manager is the inventory of your whole fleet: which accounts exist, what state they are
in, which proxy each one has and who is currently busy with a job. Preparation for any launch
starts here.

The section overview lives on the
[account manager page](https://teletraff.com/en/modules/account-manager).

## What you can do here

<Steps>

1. **Import accounts.**

   Accounts are added to the panel and land in the shared inventory.

   <figure>
     <img src="/screenshots/account-manager-table.webp" alt="TeleTraff account manager table with statuses, proxies, pools and the current module" width="1264" height="1080" loading="lazy" />
     <figcaption>Account inventory: status, bound proxy, pool and the current occupation of each account.</figcaption>
   </figure>

2. **Read each account's status.**

   The panel shows the **observed** state, not the desired one. If an account has been restricted,
   it will show here, and no job setting will change that.

3. **Filter a selection for the job.**

   Filters by state, pool, premium status and occupation let you quickly assemble a group of
   accounts fit for a specific launch.

4. **Run batch actions** on the selected accounts without opening each one.

</Steps>

## Account occupation

One account cannot run two conflicting jobs at once. If an account is busy with
[warmup](/en/modules/account-warmup/), it cannot be put on a working module — the occupation column
shows this before you build a selection.

<Aside type="tip" title="Working with a fleet">
Keep the fleet split: one part works while the other warms up. That way you never hit the situation
where every account is busy with preparation exactly when you need a launch.
</Aside>

## Preparing a new account

<Steps>

1. Import the account.
2. [Assign a proxy](/en/modules/proxy-center/) immediately — before the first action in Telegram.
3. Fill in the profile: name, avatar, bio. The panel does not do this for you.
4. Run [warmup](/en/modules/account-warmup/) matched to the account's age.
5. Only then put it on working modules.

</Steps>

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| The account went to work before a proxy was bound | it cannot be fixed retroactively |
| An empty profile | the account looks uninhabited to the platform and to people |
| A selection that ignores occupation | some accounts will not be picked up by the job |
| Expecting the panel to “fix” a restricted account | the status reflects the platform's decision |

## Limits and platform rules

The panel shows account state but does not control Telegram's decisions. A restriction imposed by
the platform is not lifted by job settings. See [Security](/en/security/).

## FAQ

**What does an account status mean?**
The observed state on Telegram's side, not the desired one. The panel does not pass an account off
as healthy if the platform thinks otherwise.

**Why can't an account be selected for a job?**
Usually it is busy with another job or has no proxy.

**Can accounts work without a proxy?**
No. Without a proxy an account will not run.

**Does the panel fill in the account profile?**
No. The name, avatar and bio are yours to fill in.
