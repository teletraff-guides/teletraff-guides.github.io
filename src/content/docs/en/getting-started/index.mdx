Every module in the panel works with three things: accounts, proxies and a dataset of targets.
If any one of them is not ready, the run either will not start or will finish empty.

## The order of preparation

<Steps>

1. **Add your accounts.**

   Accounts enter the panel through import and get an observed status.
   See the [account manager](/en/modules/account-manager/).

2. **Bind proxies.**

   A proxy is needed **before the account's first action in Telegram**, not after. Without a proxy
   the account will not run at all. See the [proxy centre](/en/modules/proxy-center/).

3. **Warm the accounts up.**

   Telegram restricts fresh and empty accounts fastest. Duration is chosen by the age of the
   account. See [warmup](/en/modules/account-warmup/).

4. **Collect a dataset of targets.**

   Inviting needs a dataset of **people**, commenting needs a list of **channels**.
   See [parsers](/en/modules/parsers/).

5. **Run one module on a small volume.**

   Before putting a task on the whole fleet, test the scenario on two or three accounts
   and a small dataset.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="What to expect from the first run">
The first run is not about results — it is a check: do actions reach Telegram, how does the target
respond and what lands in the log. Volume is increased once the picture is clear.
</Aside>
