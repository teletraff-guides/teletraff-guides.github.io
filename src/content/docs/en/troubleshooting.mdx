Most questions about a job's result come down to a handful of typical situations.

## The job finished with “Partial”

This marks a run where some targets were left deferred or unsuccessful. It is **not a failure**:
the status honestly says not everything could be closed, and the breakdown by reason is in the results.

## An account hit a FloodWait

Telegram asked it to wait. The thread pauses instead of pushing the account further.

In [inviting](/en/modules/inviting/) there are two responses: wait and continue, or stop that account
for the current job. The other accounts keep working, and the stopped account's unfinished targets
are handed to another free one — they are not lost.

An “extra pause” adds your own minutes to the delay Telegram named.

## A target was skipped

Reasons differ by module but fall into three groups.

**On the target's side.** The user has no active stories; comments are closed in the channel;
reactions are disabled for everyone; the person forbids being added to groups in their privacy settings.

**On the account's side.** The daily limit is exhausted; the account is waiting out a pause;
the account was stopped in this job; the account is not in the chat and joining is turned off.

**On the dataset's side.** The target appeared twice — no repeat action is sent.

The exact reason for each target is visible in the run results.

## The run did nothing

Nothing sent, no errors. Typical causes:

- No new posts appeared in the sources during the observation window —
  [AI commenting](/en/modules/neurocommenting/) only works with fresh posts.
- The keyword filter removed every post.
- Targets were chosen where the action is forbidden for everyone.
- The observation window is shorter than the rate at which new targets appear.

## The launch button is disabled

In [inviting](/en/modules/inviting/) the permission check runs on every selected account. Until at
least one is confirmed, launch is blocked — so the job never starts on accounts whose rights are
unknown. The hint under the button names what is missing.

## The plan is smaller than expected

Inviting has two limits: the total for the job and the maximum per account. Whichever runs out first
applies, so the actual volume equals the smaller of the two: the total limit, and “maximum per
account × number of accounts”. The gap is visible in the thread distribution block before the start.

## Comments or reactions are not going through

- The action is disabled for all participants on the target.
- The action is forbidden for this account there.
- Telegram's own limit was reached.
- Telegram requires joining the chat and joining is turned off in the job settings.
