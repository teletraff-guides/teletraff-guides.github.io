## Accounts and proxies

**Does every account need a proxy?**
Yes. Without a proxy an account will not run, and the binding is needed before the account's first
action in Telegram.

**How many accounts can go on one job?**
As many as your plan allows in concurrent threads: one account occupies one thread.

**Can other modules run during warmup?**
No. The account is busy warming up. Warm one part of the fleet while another works.

**Will warmup remove a spam block?**
No. Warmup builds a history of ordinary activity but does not undo restrictions Telegram has
already imposed.

## Runs and results

**Why did the job finish with “Partial”?**
Some targets were left deferred or unsuccessful. See [troubleshooting](/en/troubleshooting/).

**Can I stop one account without touching the others?**
Yes. Pause and stop apply to an individual line; the neighbouring accounts keep working.

**What happens to a stopped account's targets?**
Unfinished ones are handed to another free account rather than lost.

**Is a limit spent on people already in the channel?**
In inviting, no. The panel checks membership before contacting Telegram, and such a person counts
as a success without an invitation being sent.

## Language models

**Can it work without an AI provider?**
Yes, in template mode your pre-written text is sent.

**What if the model does not respond?**
The action is not performed and the reason stays in the results. No template placeholder is
substituted for a model reply.

## Telegram Premium

**Is Premium required?**
Not for ordinary work. Premium is only needed for modes where the action comes from the account's
personal channel — reactions or comments on behalf of a channel, for example.

## Security

**What pace is safe?**
There is no single threshold: it depends on the accounts' age, their history and the target. Start
with the cautious preset on a small volume and watch how Telegram responds, rather than picking
a number in advance.

**How long should an account be warmed up?**
From 30 minutes to 7 days. A fresh account needs more than half an hour — a day or more; for an
account older than a month a short preset is usually enough.
